Introduction 
Computers have changed a lot over time. In the past, they were giant, slow machines used only for hard math. In the present, they are small, fast devices like phones that we use every day. In the future, they will be super smart systems that think on their own.

Past Definition
*  An electronic or electro-mechanical machine designed purely to performing mathematical calculations and exclusively to perform complex arithmetic computations and sequential logical operations 

* Present Definition : A programmable electronic device that inputs raw data, processes it at high speeds using microprocessors, stores it electronically, and outputs meaningful information for human consumption and global communication 

Future Definition :
* An intelligent, interconnected network driven by Artificial Intelligence and quantum physics capable of understanding natural human intent, learning from its environment, and solving complex problems without human programming

Conclusion
Computers started as big tools that needed human help for everything. Today, they connect the whole world. Tomorrow, they will run our homes and cities automatically. Technology keeps growing to make our lives much easier.
